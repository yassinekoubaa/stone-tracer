# Whole-journey historical influence

Use every pre-existing v44 complete assembly (28). Each assembly supplies a fixed set of unique training occurrences. Jointly zero those examples' loss gradients once at their archived steps, retaining denominator eight; all other occurrences, optimizer settings and later minibatches are held fixed. Counterfactual parameters evolve normally. Verify the factual replay against every saved weight snapshot. No outcome-based choice of overlay or intervention.

Compare with 16 controls per case that remove the same number of examples at the exact same steps. Choose maximally disjoint subsets, prefer matching the distributions of token-position overlap with the query and ground-truth labels, then break ties randomly. Controls may coincide across repetitions; count unique schedules. Measure absolute change in target class-1 logit, sign flips, effects on the other 15 held-out inputs, and input-to-answer stone changes. Primary specificity comparison: target effect versus other-input mean, contrasted with the same difference for controls. This tests the particular complete witness returned by v44; it does not search for the most influential witness.
