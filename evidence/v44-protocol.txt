# Whole-journey overlay, fixed before inspecting outcomes

Use every saved held-out inference for seeds 104–143. For each of the 640 model/input cases, walk all 76 data stones feeding the answer against every one of the model's 960 recorded training occurrences. No intervention or branch-ranking outcome selects a match. Preserve fork roles, all parents at each merge and the actual answer. Keep the closest intact training record using the v39 continuous distance, excluding the output margin from ranking. Do not equate “closest” with a match.

Classify a whole functional correspondence only if every numerical stone, including the answer margin, aligns within v39's 0.15 distance and the predicted class agrees. Store literal input labels and mark differences: held-out input combinations necessarily differ from every archived training input. Functional origin correspondence is tested on the full learned embedding and positioned vector. Also report literal whole matches separately.

If no whole witness exists, look for a complete combination of connected historical fragments. Fixed, operation-defined fragments: the four source forks, four query–key score operations, joint softmax, four gated messages, residual merge, twelve complete nonlinear neuron branches, and the final residual merge and readout. Every data edge must appear inside at least one fragment. A donor occurrence must match every numerical stone in its fragment. Donors sharing a boundary must also agree with each other there within 0.15. Each fragment uses one actual training occurrence. All overlapping occurrences of a stone must be compatible, including at shared forks. Search for a witness; give an explicit unresolved result if the search budget of 5,000 tested assignments per case is exhausted. Missing fragment coverage is evidence only relative to this archive and matching rule. A complete assembly is evidence of numerical correspondence under the rule, not historical causal reuse.

Control: repeat both comparisons with training records made incoherent by permuting rows independently for each stone within each training minibatch, retaining values, operation slots, checkpoint and candidate count. Positive check: exact replay of a stored training occurrence must qualify as a literal whole match. Report continuous overlays, connected stretches, breaks and later convergence even when complete recognition fails. Keep seeds separate in archives and diagnostics.
